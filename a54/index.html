answers to all your questions

Do Deep Sleep Stack gummies work?
Deep Sleep Stack is formulated to help tight muscles relax and support deeper, more restful sleep. Magnesium plays a role in normal muscle relaxation and nerve signaling, and lots of people take it before bed as part of their routine. Results vary from person to person.*
What's in Deep Sleep Stack?
Magnesium glycinate, potassium and L-theanine in a vegan, gelatin-free wild berry pectin gummy.
Is there melatonin in it?
No. The formula is built around magnesium, potassium and L-theanine — no melatonin, so no groggy mornings from it.
Why magnesium glycinate?
Glycinate is a gentle, well-absorbed form of magnesium that's easy on the stomach — a popular choice for an evening routine.*
When should I take Deep Sleep Stack?
Chew 2 gummies 30–60 minutes before bed. Keep the jar on your nightstand so it becomes a habit.
How many gummies should I take?
Two gummies a night. One jar holds 60 gummies — a one-month supply.
How fast does it work?
Everyone is different. Magnesium works best as a consistent nightly habit, so give it a few weeks of regular use.*
Will I feel groggy in the morning?
There's no melatonin or sedatives in the formula, so it's designed to let you wake up clear.*
Is Deep Sleep Stack habit forming?
It's a magnesium supplement with no melatonin and no sedatives. Talk to your doctor if you have questions about your own situation.
Can I take it every night?
Yes, it's designed as a nightly routine. Don't exceed 2 gummies a day.
What do the gummies taste like?
Wild berry — sweet, a little tart, no chalky aftertaste.
Are the gummies vegan?
Yes. They're made with fruit pectin instead of gelatin.
Are they gluten-free?
Yes, the formula contains no gluten ingredients.
How much sugar is in a serving?
About 2 g per 2-gummy serving, with no corn syrup.
Can I take it with other supplements or medication?
Talk to your doctor before taking any supplement if you take medication or have a medical condition.
Is it safe during pregnancy or breastfeeding?
Please talk to your doctor before taking any supplement if you're pregnant or nursing.
Who is Deep Sleep Stack for?
Adults who want a melatonin-free way to help their muscles relax and support better sleep.*
